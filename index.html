About

I am a Ph.D. candidate at HKUST(GZ), supervised by Luoye Chen and co-supervised by Chao Zhang. My research draws on computational social science to study cities, digital infrastructure, and supply chains.

Previously, I studied Statistics and Data Science at SUSTech with Bingyi Jing, and Physics at Shenzhen University.
